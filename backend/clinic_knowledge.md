## Expanded patient-concierge knowledge (reviewed 19 November 2026)

### Clinic identity and philosophy
Mert Demirel Clinic is a plastic, reconstructive and aesthetic surgery practice in Istanbul, Türkiye. The official website presents Op. ### What a consultation involves
Consultation is an evaluation and decision process, not a sales pitch or an automatic commitment. The published approach is to listen to goals, examine anatomy/tissue quality/proportions/function, explain the mechanism behind the concern, discuss options and alternatives (including doing nothing), and review risks, limitations and recovery. The patient is not obliged to proceed. Two people asking for the same procedure may have different anatomy and needs.
Source: https://mertdemirel.com/about/approach/

### Breast procedures
Published areas include augmentation, lift (mastopexy), reduction, implant removal, implant exchange/revision, areola reduction and gynecomastia surgery.
- Augmentation addresses volume and proportion; planning considers tissue, skin envelope, breast base, chest wall, implant dimensions and long-term stability.
- A lift addresses breast position and the skin envelope; a larger implant does not automatically correct sagging.
- Revision investigates why the previous result is unsatisfactory: implant position, pocket mechanics, capsule behaviour or changes in the skin envelope. It may involve more than exchanging an implant.
- Implants are not lifetime devices in the practical sense; future monitoring or surgery may be needed. Never promise a cup size or a result identical to a photograph.
Sources: https://mertdemirel.com/breast-procedures/ and https://mertdemirel.com/procedures/breast-augmentation/

### Face and nose
Published facial areas include primary, revision and functional rhinoplasty; eyelid surgery/blepharoplasty; facelift (deep-plane, SMAS and mini facelift); midface lift; neck lift; brow lift; lip lift; otoplasty; bichectomy; alar base reduction; canthopexy/canthoplasty; cheek augmentation; cheekbone reduction; chin augmentation and chin liposuction. Rhinoplasty considers nasal structure and may also involve breathing function. Facial rejuvenation considers skin, volume, connective tissue and deeper support, aiming to preserve identity and balance.
Source: https://mertdemirel.com/facial-procedures/

### Body contouring
Published areas include liposuction, abdominal/waist/flank/thigh liposuction, tummy tuck/abdominoplasty (mini, 360-degree and fleur-de-lis approaches), panniculectomy, umbilicoplasty, arm lift, thigh lift, body lift, mommy makeover and BBL.
- Liposuction reduces selected subcutaneous fat; it is not weight-loss surgery and does not remove visceral fat or a large loose-skin envelope.
- Tummy tuck addresses redundant abdominal skin and, when indicated, selected abdominal-wall laxity; it is not a weight-loss operation and cannot guarantee a scarless or permanently flat abdomen.
- Body contouring considers different tissue layers: fat, skin envelope, abdominal wall and regional proportions. One operation does not solve every layer.
Sources: https://mertdemirel.com/body-procedures/ ; https://mertdemirel.com/procedures/liposuction/ ; https://mertdemirel.com/procedures/tummy-tuck/
