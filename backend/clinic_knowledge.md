# Mert Demirel Clinic — public website knowledge snapshot
Source website: https://mertdemirel.com/
This file is a curated snapshot of information publicly displayed on the official website. It is not a substitute for a clinician's individual advice. If a fact is not present here, do not guess; direct the visitor to the clinic team or the relevant official page.

## Practice and surgeon
- Op. Dr. Mert Demirel is presented as a Plastic, Reconstructive & Aesthetic Surgeon practising in Istanbul.
- The website describes his approach as anatomy-first, proportion-focused, conservative, and centred on natural identity and long-term stability.
- The website states that he completed medical education and plastic surgery training at Ankara University, received advanced microsurgery training at the University of Zurich, received his specialization in 2010, received the EBOPRAS European Board Certificate in 2015, and established his own private practice in 2016.
- The website identifies him as EBOPRAS certified and a member of ISAPS and ASPS. Do not expand these credentials beyond what the site states.
- The site describes academic work including the Chestnut Technique in breast surgery.

## Clinical philosophy
- The site says aesthetic surgery should begin with understanding the patient's concern, anatomy, indication, alternatives, risks, and realistic expectations—not simply choosing a procedure name.
- It emphasizes that not every concern requires surgery and not every procedure is appropriate for every person.
- It describes a selective, individualized approach rather than standard packages or pressure to proceed.
- It says the surgeon may advise against an operation or suggest waiting when appropriate.
- Never promise a perfect, guaranteed, permanent, or specific result.

## Consultation and international patient journey
- The website describes an individualized consultation involving discussion of goals, examination, measurements, anatomy, realistic options, limitations and alternatives. It says there is no obligation to proceed.
- For international patients, the site describes multilingual communication, pre-operative evaluation, hospital coordination, transfer and accommodation coordination, postoperative checks and remote follow-up.
- The site says surgical procedures are carried out in cooperation with well-equipped hospitals in Istanbul. Do not invent or name a hospital.
- The clinic's contact team can answer initial questions, gather information for an eligibility assessment and guide the next step. Only the qualified clinical team can assess suitability.
- Travel timing, length of stay, and fitness to fly depend on the specific procedure, the individual patient and recovery. Never give a universal number of days or clear someone to fly.

## Preparation, recovery and safety
- The official Patient Guide covers consultation, patient safety, pre-operative preparation, international patients, travel to Istanbul, recovery, post-operative care, follow-up and warning signs.
- Pre-operative preparation may involve medical history, tests/reports, medication and supplement review, nicotine considerations, fasting instructions, and practical support arrangements. Exact instructions must come from the treating clinical and anaesthesia teams.
- Patients must not stop prescribed medication based on chatbot advice.
- Recovery varies by procedure, individual health, previous surgery, tissue response and daily demands. Follow the surgeon's individual postoperative instructions.
- Follow-up is part of care. International patients should ask which checks need to happen before departure and how remote follow-up will work.
- For concerning postoperative changes, advise contacting the treating clinic/clinical team. For severe or rapidly worsening symptoms or an emergency, seek local emergency medical care immediately rather than waiting for a chatbot reply.
